# MatCheck Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-06-06

MatCheck ("we", "our", "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices when using the MatCheck mobile application.

## Information We Collect

- Account information: email address and basic profile information you provide.
- User content: profile details, messages, reports, announcements, calendar-related content, and uploaded media you submit in the app.
- Device and usage data: app diagnostics, crash logs, and technical information needed to operate and improve app performance.

## How We Use Information

- Provide core app features such as authentication, profiles, chat, school/sport discovery, announcements, and reporting.
- Maintain app security, detect abuse, and enforce community rules.
- Troubleshoot issues and improve reliability and performance.
- Comply with legal obligations.

## Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information.

We may share data with trusted service providers that help us operate the app (for example, backend hosting and authentication/storage infrastructure), and when required by law.

## Data Retention

We retain information as long as needed to provide services, fulfill legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements. Retention periods may vary by data type and legal requirements.

## Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect your information. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

## Children's Privacy

MatCheck is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required by local law). If you believe a child has provided personal data, contact us and we will take appropriate action.

## Your Choices

You may request access, correction, or deletion of your data where applicable by contacting us.
