# MatCheck Account Deletion

Effective date: 2026-06-06

If you want to delete your MatCheck account and associated personal data, submit a deletion request using one of the methods below.

## Request Deletion

- Email: privacy@matcheck.app
- One-click email link: mailto:privacy@matcheck.app?subject=MatCheck%20Account%20Deletion%20Request

Please include the following in your request:

- Your account email address
- Your full name
- A short confirmation that you want your account deleted

## What Happens After You Request Deletion

- We will verify ownership of the account before processing deletion.
- We will delete or anonymize account and profile data associated with your account.
- Content you created in shared spaces (for example, messages, announcements, or reports) may be removed or anonymized based on system and legal requirements.

## Retention Exceptions

Some data may be retained when required for:

- Legal compliance
- Fraud and abuse prevention
- Security and dispute resolution

## Processing Time

We aim to complete verified deletion requests within 30 days.

## Questions

For account deletion or privacy questions, contact privacy@matcheck.app.
